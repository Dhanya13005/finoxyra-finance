## ✨ What is Finoxyra?

Finoxyra is a personal finance web app for Indian users. You add your salary and your monthly spending, and it shows how much you are really saving.

Want to buy a car or a house? Enter the price and a target date, and Finoxyra tells you how much to put aside each month to get there.

## 🚀 Features

- 🔐 **Secure sign up and login** with JWT and encrypted passwords
- 💵 **Income tracking** for salary, bonus, freelancing and more
- 🧾 **Expense tracking** by category: household, food, health, entertainment and children's education
- 📊 **Dashboard** with savings rate and a spending breakdown chart
- 🎯 **Goal planner** that shows the monthly saving needed for a car, house or anything else

## 🧱 How it works

`React (Vercel)` → `Spring Boot API (Render)` → `MySQL-compatible database (TiDB Cloud)`

## ⏳ Good to know

The backend runs on a free hosting plan. If the app has been idle, the **first login can take a minute or two** while the server wakes up. After that it is fast.

🚧 **Coming soon:** income, expense and goal data is saved in your browser for now. Cloud sync through the backend is next.
